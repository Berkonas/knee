# Project brief (summary)

The course's Project 2 handout (Biomechanics of Human Movement, Fall 2026) is the instructor's document and is
**not redistributed in this public repository**. Below is our own short summary of the requirements that shaped
this app.

* Act as the product company's internal R&D team (our scenario: **TrailBuddy trekking poles**). Investigate whether
  the poles reduce load on the legs during downhill hiking without excessive arm and shoulder demand, and whether
  a pole length or technique works best.
* Run an original small human-participant experiment comparing product and control conditions.
* Estimate **at least one internal musculoskeletal quantity** and **at least one secondary effect** elsewhere in
  the body. A kinematics-only comparison is not enough.
* Justify the measurements, the model assumptions, and how unmeasured external forces were estimated.
* Do not overstate: this is a preliminary study. Report differences under the tested conditions with their
  uncertainties, and don't equate lower estimated load with less pain, fatigue or injury.
* The final report includes an executive summary, methods, estimates, uncertainties, secondary effects, practical
  interpretation and candidate customer-facing claims labelled as preliminary.

Our protocol: `TrailBuddy_Experimental_Protocol_Berk.docx` / `..._Revised.pdf` in this folder.
